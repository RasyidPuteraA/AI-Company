# INTERNAL-061: Add Autonomy Operating Policy

## Goal

Add formal operating policy for client approval gates, idle internal improvement mode, and daily/3-day/weekly reporting expectations.

## Implemented

Added:

- `projects/internal/ai-company-os/AUTONOMY_OPERATING_POLICY.md`

## Policy Summary

- Client projects may run autonomously until Owner review.
- Client projects must wait for Owner approval before finalization.
- Daily, 3-day, and weekly reports are required operating cadences.
- When there are no active client orders, agents may improve internal infrastructure and the virtual office.
- Client work has priority over internal improvement work.
