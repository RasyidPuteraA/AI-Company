FAQ

Common workshop questions.

Can VoltForge service any electric vehicle brand?

This fictional workshop demo is positioned for common EV diagnostics, battery health reviews, charging checks, and fleet maintenance across modern electric platforms.

Do you need the vehicle before preparing a diagnostic bay?

The service desk can prepare the bay from the request details, then confirm the exact checklist after intake and initial high-voltage safety checks.

What does the battery health report include?

Reports summarize pack condition, cell balance indicators, thermal behavior, range guidance, and clear priority levels for recommended follow-up work.

Can fleet operators schedule multiple vehicles?

Yes. Fleet requests can be staged around priority bay access, downtime planning, and repeatable service history tracking for each vehicle.
